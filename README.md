# Hi, I'm Maria Driscoll

Marketing and customer success leader who builds the tools her teams run on.

I have more than 15 years across marketing, business development and customer success, in public health, medical technology, executive consulting and B2B technology. The common thread is behaviour change: getting busy, sceptical professionals to try something new and keep using it. Lately I also build the tools behind that work, from adoption dashboards to AI agents.

**Portfolio:** [mariadriscoll-builds.github.io](https://mariadriscoll-builds.github.io) · **LinkedIn:** [maria-driscoll](https://www.linkedin.com/in/maria-driscoll)

## Selected work

- **[Client adoption dashboard](https://mariadriscoll-builds.github.io/demos/adoption-dashboard.html)**: a daily record of whether each customer actually uses the product, drawn as a heatmap that shows an account slipping away at a glance.
- **[Client account records](https://mariadriscoll-builds.github.io/demos/account-records.html)**: one page per account with what we owe them, what they owe us, and the date every fact was last confirmed.
- **[The morning run](https://mariadriscoll-builds.github.io/demos/morning-run.html)**: scheduled AI agents that keep every client's working file current, and the rules that stop them writing confident nonsense.
- **[Logins aren't usage](https://mariadriscoll-builds.github.io/demos/logins-arent-usage.html)**: an evidence report showing why a product's activity numbers couldn't be trusted, with ranked asks for engineering.
- **[Bonus gap calculator](https://mariadriscoll-builds.github.io/demos/bonus-calculator.html)**: a gated ROI calculator, plus the [Cloudflare Worker relay](https://mariadriscoll-builds.github.io/code/form-relay-worker.js) behind it that blocks spam and captures the prospect's real numbers.

Every demo uses fictional clients, people and figures. The design, logic and writing are my own.
